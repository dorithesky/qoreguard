What the owner would have to sign

For the raise-cap action, the contract builds this exact byte string (adminMessage(action, payload, nonce)) and hands it to the pqcVerify precompile. It binds the domain, the chain id, this contract, the action, the payload and the nonce — so a signature for one action cannot become another, and one for another deployment cannot be replayed here.

The owner's signature, verified in your browser

ML-DSA-87 verification, against the key the chain holds

A recorded message signature from the owner's key is checked here with verifyMessageWithChain from @qorechain/qoresign/network — the same call a dApp makes after window.qorex.native.signArbitrary. The ML-DSA-87 key is read from the chain's pqcKeyStatus precompile (0x…0a02), the one the contract itself calls.
